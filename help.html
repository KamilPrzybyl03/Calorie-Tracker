Recommended Workouts

  • High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT): 20-30 minutes, 8-12 intervals of 2-3 minutes at 90-100% of your maximum heart rate.
  • Steady-State Cardio: 30-60 minutes, at a moderate intensity (60-70% of your maximum heart rate).
  • Strength Training: 30-60 minutes, targeting major muscle groups (e.g., chest, back, legs).

Gym Routines

  • Full Body Workout: 30 minutes, consisting of cardio (e.g., jogging, cycling), strength training (e.g., push-ups, squats), and flexibility exercises (e.g., yoga).
  • Upper Body Workout: 20 minutes, targeting the chest, shoulders, and triceps.
  • Lower Body Workout: 20 minutes, targeting the legs, glutes, and core.
